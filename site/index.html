PiP Camera for Windows

pip-view is a free picture-in-picture webcam viewer. It shows your camera in a small window that stays on top of all other apps, and it shows only the area of the camera that you select.

Download pip-view.exe Source code on GitHub

Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit. No installation. Free and open source (MIT).

Features

  • Always on top. The webcam window stays above your other apps and has no border.
  • Crop the camera. Show only a part of the image: a whiteboard, a desk, a door, or a second screen.
  • Move and resize. Drag the window to move it. Drag the bottom-right corner to resize it.
  • Colored border. None, White, Green, Red, Blue, or Yellow.
  • Any camera. USB webcams, laptop cameras, capture cards, and virtual cameras.
  • Remembers settings. The camera, the area, and the window position stay the same at the next start.

How to use the PiP webcam window

  1. Download and start pip-view.exe.
  2. Select your camera and click Next.
  3. Drag a rectangle on the camera image to select the area. Click Confirm.
  4. The picture-in-picture window opens. Right-click it to select a different area, a different camera, or a border.

Questions

How do I show my webcam in a picture-in-picture window on Windows?

Download pip-view.exe and start it. Select your camera, drag a rectangle on the camera image, and click Confirm. The camera opens in a small window that stays on top of other apps.

Can I show only a part of the camera image?

Yes. pip-view crops the camera image to the area that you select. Right-click the window and click Select area to change it.

Is pip-view free?

Yes. pip-view is free and open source under the MIT license.

Do I need to install pip-view?

No. pip-view is 1 .exe file. Download it and run it.
